About Me

Hi there, I'm Jake. I'm a student at Lansing Community College and I learn all about computers! I have a blind dog and two cats.
I spend most of my time doing assignments for classes, watching The Middle, and listening to music.

My Goals

I began at Lansing Community College hoping that one day I would become a game developer. Now, I still hope that could
be possible, but I'm open to alternative options. The main reason I want to become a game developer is to share my art and stories.
Since I am capable with computer programming, it is one of my only ways I can share my art and stories.

My Art

I consider art to be one of the most important things in life, and I create art quite frequently. I've found that
there are many different mediums of art that I like to work with, including drawing, digital art, needle felting, clay, photography,
and I've even found Minecraft to be a great art medium.

Food Fighters
When I was very young, I thought up a food battle game where food would go
head to head with moldy zombie foods. This drawing (created using Procreate) was inspired
by the old concept art I made as a child.

Class Doodles
During my Creative Writing class in high school, I was tasked with writing a short story.
In order for me to write a story, I have to visualize it. Thus, these doodles were born!

The Forsaken Sponge of Dread

The theme was Spongebob Squarepants, and for some unknown reason, I created this... thing.

Forever Whole
As previously mentioned, I had to write a short story for my high school class. This image (created
